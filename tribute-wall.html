Jasper: The Mountain Trail Guide

"When we adopted Jasper from the shelter ten years ago, he was timid and afraid of storms. With patience, he transformed into our family's fearless trail leader, summiting over 40 peaks across the Pacific Northwest. In his final months, when his paws grew tired, he still loved smelling the pine trees from his wagon. Everlasting Paws captured his spirit so beautifully with our memorial glass stone."
