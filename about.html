Our History

Founded on Compassion, Built on Unbroken Trust

When our founder lost her childhood dog Oliver in 2012, she realized how sterile, cold, and transactional traditional pet aftercare could be. She set out to build a genuine sanctuary where grief is honored with warmth, gentle music, and unconditional dignity.

Today, Everlasting Paws serves pet parents across the region with private viewing suites, dedicated individual cremation chambers, handcrafted keepsakes, and free bereavement support groups.
